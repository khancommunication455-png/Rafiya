Please put your assets in this 'public' folder. 

1. name your music 'music.mp3'
2. name your notification sound 'notification.mp3'
3. name your background/story images: 
   - '1.jpg' (Intro background)
   - '2.jpg', '3.jpg', '4.jpg', '5.jpg', '6.jpg' (Memory Gallery photos)

Vite will automatically serve these files from the root. 
Example: '/music.mp3' will point to 'public/music.mp3'.
